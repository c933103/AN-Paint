## Per-key decisions

### string/ui_fit — retain

English: Fit
Candidate: በመስኮቱ ልክ
Confidence: medium-high (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The window-sized view phrase fits a viewport zoom/position command. It does not instruct image resampling or selected-image placement. This is an acceptable contextual expansion of the short English Fit.

Status, navigation-options and bounds-editor buttons call paintCanvas.fit(). The fitted rectangle is the union of bitmap canvas, trim rectangle and floating-selection geometry; padding is reserved around handles. Only zoom/pan and view state are adjusted. A too-small viewport returns early.

Evidence class: Exact nonfuzzy LibreOffice phrase transferred from Fit To Window; not an exact English source-pair match.

Limits: Attested wording comes from a desktop-window command; AN Paint suitability is contextual review, not native-user acceptance. Do not describe Fit as exclusively canvas bounds; outlying trim/floating selection bounds are included. Do not confuse ui_fit with ui_fit_selection34, which changes selected-image placement.

### string/ui_fit_entire_canvas_in_view — hold

English: Fit entire canvas in view
Candidate: የመሳያ ቦታውን በሙሉ አሳይ
Confidence: low-medium (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The draft amounts to showing the whole drawing area. Drawing area can denote the visible editor/viewport rather than the bitmap canvas, and the fit-to-view relation is not securely expressed. The descriptive fallback is not adequate evidence for this precision-sensitive accessibility caption.

The Fit status control has this contentDescription, and EditIcon.FIT uses it as its accessible label and tooltip. It names the same viewport-only Fit operation, including the entire bitmap plus outlying trim/floating-selection bounds.

Evidence class: New composition, not an attested complete caption or independently established technical term for canvas.

Limits: Do not implement this wording or silently promote the draft alternative. Do not infer English is wrong merely because fitting includes outliers: entire canvas is inclusive, not canvas-only. Resolve the canvas-versus-viewport noun and fit relation with defensible lexical/native evidence before acceptance.

### string/ui_draw_again — retain

English: Draw again
Candidate: ድጋሚ ሳል
Confidence: medium (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The phrase is a direct user-directed draw-again imperative faithful to the existing English. It does not itself say replay, redraw automatically, or undo/redo. In the navigation panel it can conventionally invite resuming drawing work. The alternative return-to-tool composition would change the source framing and is not silently substituted.

The button calls chooseTool(drawingTool). drawingTool defaults to PENCIL and is remembered for every non-ZOOM tool, including SELECT, LASSO, FILL and PICKER. chooseTool selects the Draw panel and calls selectTool; selectTool calls applyPending before switching. No new stroke is generated by pressing the button.

Evidence class: Exact Android user-redraw wording, transferred from lock-pattern drawing, not repaint/re-render.

Limits: Context confidence remains medium because the remembered tool can be selection/fill/picker rather than a freehand drawing tool. This is not proof that English Draw again is maximally precise; it is sufficient for a faithful current-source translation. If product owners decide the command should say Return to previous tool, revise canonical English and flag/review every affected language rather than fixing only Amharic. Do not claim the tool switch is mutation-free or cannot commit pending content.

### string/ui_pixel_grid33 — retain

English: Pixel grid
Candidate: የፒክሰል ፍርግርግ
Confidence: medium (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The genitive pixel-grid compound is semantically transparent and names the displayed grid. Neither the noun phrase nor its modifier adds snapping, resolution changes or pixelation.

The View command toggles paintCanvas.grid. The grid setter updates ruler/viewport geometry and may recenter/refit the view; display lines are drawn on the view canvas only when grid is true and zoom >= 8. Rulers are drawn whenever grid is true, independently of that zoom threshold. No grid is baked into the document bitmap.

Evidence class: New pixel-plus-grid noun phrase from separately attested Android vocabulary; not exact full-phrase reuse.

Limits: The complete compound is composed, not an exact attested UI phrase. Do not describe both grid and rulers as visible only at 800%; only grid lines have that threshold. Grid toggling pauses an active gesture through pauseGesture, so avoid an unqualified promise that it has no state effects.

### string/ui_fullscreen33 — retain

English: Full screen
Candidate: ሙሉ ገጽ ዕይታ
Confidence: medium-high (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The established full-screen-view noun phrase works as a View-mode command. It describes display presentation without instructing image resizing or cropping.

The command toggles fullscreen and calls syncFullscreen. That hides non-workspace root children, sidebar and status/ribbon rails, adds a leave-fullscreen button, and sets SYSTEM_UI_FLAG_FULLSCREEN. Restoring the mode is reversible and does not alter document image dimensions.

Evidence class: Exact Android Full screen phrase; transferred between full-screen mode contexts.

Limits: The Android source record is an aspect-ratio/full-screen display setting, not this editor implementation. SYSTEM_UI_FLAG_FULLSCREEN does not by itself establish immersive navigation-bar hiding. No device runtime, system-bar or layout verification was performed.

### string/ui_show_controls — retain

English: Show controls
Candidate: መቆጣጠሪያዎችን አሳይ
Confidence: medium-high (translation); high (consumer semantics).

Removing the device qualifier leaves the plural controls object, its accusative marking and the show imperative intact. It is an apt direct instruction for making editor controls visible again, without importing device-settings meaning.

The workspace return button sets fullscreen=false and calls syncFullscreen. It restores controls/rails subject to their existing panel visibility state, removes the return button and resets the system-UI flag.

Evidence class: Derived shortening of Android Show device controls by removing the device qualifier; not an exact full-phrase citation.

Limits: No claim that the complete shortened string is verbatim in Android. Show controls means leaving full-screen, not opening a control-customization dialog or exposing every collapsed panel.

### string/ui_navigate — retain

English: Navigate
Candidate: እይታ ማንቀሳቀሻ
Confidence: medium (translation); high (consumer semantics).

The view modifier makes the moved object the view rather than drawing content. A tool noun is suitable for the tool tile and label. This contextual choice is clearer for one-finger viewport panning than importing general menu navigation terminology merely because the English word matches.

PaintTool.ZOOM binds ui_navigate; ToolButton uses its label for text, contentDescription and tooltip. chooseTool selects the View panel and selectTool applies pending trim/polygon/curve/floating content before changing tool. In the resulting mode, ordinary one-finger drag adjusts panX/panY and a single tap does not draw or change zoom; two-finger pinch remains a separate zoom/pan path.

Evidence class: Composed view-movement tool noun using a nonfuzzy LibreOffice pan-tool noun and an Android-attested view noun; not an exact Navigate phrase.

Limits: The complete compound is newly composed, not exact phrase attestation or native-speaker acceptance. Existing inherited project labels use the spelling ዕይታ; Android also attests እይታ. This orthographic variation alone does not establish a semantic defect; inherited zoom translations are out of scope. Do not infer that selecting Navigate is mutation-free merely because subsequent dragging pans. The mode also permits two-finger zooming; this short label is not an exhaustive gesture instruction.
