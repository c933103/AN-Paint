## Common consumer meaning

Actual tab order View, Draw, File, Edit, Color. makeHeader maps fixed internal English identifiers through menuTitle to localized labels. RibbonTab text and contentDescription receive the label; click calls selectTab. selectTab only sets activeTab, expands sidebar, syncs panels and refreshes custom palette for Color. syncPanels controls visibility/selection and persists sidebar expansion; fullscreen can still suppress the panel. Tab selection does not execute menuActions, choose a tool, or change bitmap/color. Tool buttons may separately switch tabs through chooseTool. RibbonTab declares ActionBar.Tab accessibility class/selected state, but no screen-reader validation is claimed.
