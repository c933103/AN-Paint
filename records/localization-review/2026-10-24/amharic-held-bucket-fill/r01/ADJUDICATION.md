# Amharic Bucket fill independent adjudication

## Decision

Release the linguistic hold on **string/ui_bucket_fill** and retain **ቀለም መሙያ** unchanged for English **Bucket fill**. This is acceptance of this one short caption as ready for implementation, not an application release, production edit, or acceptance of its surrounding strings.

Functional fidelity is **medium-high**. Contemporary idiom and standalone accessible-name preference remain **medium and not user-tested**. The decision does not claim native-speaker certification or an exact historical bucket-tool use of the whole phrase.

## Why the new evidence resolves the hold

The [previous review](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/tree/f3ce344991cbf95a1e629386c39723b9da537fec/records/localization-review/2026-10-24/amharic-selection-tools/r01) identified a real gap: LibreOffice attested a Fill Color control, not a flood-fill tool. That record alone did not establish transfer to the target operation. The new evidence addresses the missing operation and grammatical relation rather than merely adding more occurrences of the original property label.

1. [Tux Paint’s historical active records](https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxpaint/tuxpaint/ci/6633b40d7fb92e36a100f38d4c418a1fee768678/tree/src/po/am.po#l913) give the fill-tool name መሙላት and the area-with-colour relation ቦታ በቀለም ለመሙላት. I inspected the full same-revision fill source: its [name, instruction and click callback](https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxpaint/tuxpaint/ci/6633b40d7fb92e36a100f38d4c418a1fee768678/tree/magic/src/fill.c#l101) lead to [connected horizontal runs and adjacent-row traversal](https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxpaint/tuxpaint/ci/6633b40d7fb92e36a100f38d4c418a1fee768678/tree/magic/src/fill.c#l194). This is actual raster flood filling, not choosing a colour or changing a shape property. Different RGB matching details do not invalidate that operation-level comparison, and are not imported into AN Paint.
2. The [current non-fuzzy Tux Paint Fill record](https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxpaint/tuxpaint/ci/8ec0dc4e1554955df3f5753b6b99bf2569bfae7a/tree/src/po/am.po#l1062), its [tool-name consumer](https://sourceforge.net/p/tuxpaint/tuxpaint/ci/8ec0dc4e1554955df3f5753b6b99bf2569bfae7a/tree/src/tools.h#l78), and the [official description of contiguous-area filling](https://tuxpaint.org/docs/en/html/README.html#using_tools_drawing_fill) corroborate this domain. Historical and current versions of one translation are not counted as separate independent authorities. The original instruction is admitted only at its active historical revision. Its fuzzy current descendants and the current obsolete copy do not supply current acceptance evidence.
3. The visually inspected [instrumental discussion](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/pluginfile.php/6774/mod_resource/content/0/amharic_rg.pdf#page=232) supports naming an action’s means or tool. The [nominal and verbal uses](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/pluginfile.php/6774/mod_resource/content/0/amharic_rg.pdf#page=233) also show why an instrumental need not be a physical implement. Applying that analysis to መሙያ is a linguistic inference, not a direct grammar quotation of this software term. The [head-final noun-phrase description](https://aclanthology.org/2004.eamt-1.6.pdf#page=3) supports the order of the analysis, not the idiomaticity of an arbitrary compound.
4. The complete spelling and compact colour-plus-filling sequence are independently attested in [LibreOffice’s command caption](https://github.com/LibreOffice/translations/blob/681a7f1e79cd6e27496259dab7940c8e490059de/source/am/officecfg/registry/data/org/openoffice/Office/UI.po#L21955-L21963). Thus the decision does not invent a new word sequence solely from general grammar. Tux Paint supplies the otherwise missing same-operation relation; grammar supports the means/tool reading; LibreOffice establishes the complete phrase’s software use.

This combined evidence supports the contextual reading “colour-filling tool.” That is a functional translation of the verified operation. A short caption need not reproduce the bucket metaphor or explain connectivity, tolerance, and undo. The phrase retains filling-action content when presented without the icon; it is not merely a colour noun or an instruction to choose a colour. This is a semantic assessment of the caption, not a claim that screen-reader users have been tested.

## Counterevidence and alternatives

LibreOffice’s [Fillcolor property record](https://github.com/LibreOffice/translations/blob/681a7f1e79cd6e27496259dab7940c8e490059de/source/am/svx/messages.po#L1822-L1826) uses exactly the same phrase. Its [documented object-area colour behavior](https://help.libreoffice.org/latest/am/text/sdraw/main0210.html#hd_id361683306526098) is not flood filling. That is genuine ambiguity evidence, and the instrumental analysis does not make the phrase exclusively tool-specific: a means or filling material can also be named by an instrumental. The remaining uncertainty is therefore substantive, not erased by a grammar rule.

It does not, however, demonstrate that the phrase can only name a setting or that it misidentifies AN Paint’s tool. Both readings preserve a colour-filling function. In the verified standalone tool role, the operation-bearing wording is sufficient for this bounded caption review. No competing colour/shape control was found to consume this key. Claiming zero ambiguity would be unsupported; continuing the hold solely because the whole phrase lacks an earlier bucket caption would impose an unnecessary exact-match gate.

The directly attested alternative **መሙላት** is credible and shorter, but still generic filling. The evidence does not demonstrate a functional or tool-versus-setting clarity gain sufficient to require replacing the existing caption. **በቀለም መሙያ** and **ቦታ በቀለም መሙያ** make the relation more explicit, but are new compositions and are not shown to be more idiomatic. The [glossary’s colour-application alternative](https://oromianeconomist.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/ict-glossary-in-afaan-oromoo-amharic-and-tigarinya.pdf#page=104) belongs to a closed-shape colour control and has no better flood-fill precedent. None warrants a forced revision.

## Source contract checked

The production ref was independently re-read during this adjudication and still matches the [reviewed English source](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/res/values/strings.xml#L67). All 18 complete supplied AN Paint files independently matched the recorded full production tree. The actual consumer chain, not only the author’s summary, was inspected. The [Amharic production catalogue](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/res/values-b+am/strings.xml) still lacks this key.

- The [FILL enum](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L14-L36) and [primary toolbar](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ClassicPaintActivity.kt#L286-L299) establish a standalone tool, distinct from shape styles and foreground/background colour selection. [ToolButton](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ToolButton.kt#L10-L18) uses the caption as visible text, a standalone content description and a short tooltip. FILL is not a category member. Help, heading and status compositions remain separate reviews.
- The ordinary [pointer-up route](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L795-L816) passes final image coordinates converted to integers. The release point is the seed, not a continuous fill stroke. Multitouch, rulers, scrollbars and cancellation have separate handling; the relative drawing cursor does not add a Fill path.
- [PaintDocument.fill](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L260-L269) bounds-checks the seed and skips an identical foreground seed only at zero tolerance. Otherwise it checkpoints, fills and marks dirty. The [algorithm](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ScanlineFill.kt#L12-L54) fills the orthogonally seed-connected matching region. It compares to the original seed using exact colour equality at zero threshold or squared RGBA distance against squared integer tolerance. Disconnected matching pixels are not globally replaced. The UI tolerance is 0–100, not an area percentage.
- The [foreground setter](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L43-L58) forces opaque alpha. Alpha is considered in matching, but fill does not promise preserved transparency. Checkpointing clears redo; the error branch attempts raster restoration and removes the new checkpoint, without a claim of complete history rollback.
- Every [tool choice](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L153-L157) calls [applyPending](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L443-L449), which calls finishSelection. Pending geometry or floating selections may commit, including when choosing a selection tool or reselecting the current tool. The caption review does not describe tool choice as non-mutating.

No English defect was established. Keep English **Bucket fill** unchanged. A translation evidence question does not justify silently changing the source to Fill Color.
