## Why the original hold is now resolved

The original hold had two substantive gaps: a generic drawing-area noun could mean the visible editor area, and merely showing that area omitted the fitting relation. The revision repairs both. It names the canvas as the displayed object, expresses its entirety, supplies a fit-to-window relation and ends with a show command. It does not silently change the English to a different operation. [Historical review](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/tree/fe0657e03789ca58d9cf786d1da6093296612fda/records/localization-review/2026-10-24/amharic-view-controls/r01) · [Canonical source](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/res/values/strings.xml#L199)

The ICT Development Office/ELRC technical glossary explicitly gives **የስዕል ሸራ** for drawing canvas at entry 1011, page 85. This establishes a technical canvas term, not just a loose physical-material analogy. The shorter head noun is adequate in this painting application; retaining the whole compound is not necessary to distinguish canvas from viewport. The glossary's vector-container example supports the lexical distinction, not an assertion that AN Paint uses the same object model. Shull's page 8 independently corroborates the canvas noun. [Technical glossary, p.85](https://oromianeconomist.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/ict-glossary-in-afaan-oromoo-amharic-and-tigarinya.pdf#page=85) · [Shull, p.8](https://conference.usu.edu/SYSTEM/Uploads/Handouts/15233_1981miriamredleaf.pdf#page=8)

These are evidence for the relation, not proof that the assembled caption is an existing quotation. ## Grammar and whole-caption judgment

- **ሸራውን** is a defensible definite direct-object form: vowel-final noun, definite suffix, then object marking. This is productive morphology, not an alleged example quoted from a grammar. The consulted references support those morphological steps. [Beermann and Ephrem, section 2.2, p.23](https://web.stanford.edu/group/cslipublications/cslipublications/TLS/TLS9-2005/TLS9_Beermann_and_Ephrem.pdf#page=3) · [Leslau, section 16, printed pp.47–48](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/pluginfile.php/6774/mod_resource/content/0/amharic_rg.pdf#page=64)
- **በሙሉ** conveys the canvas in its entirety. It does not mean that only the canvas may be included, or that the application should enter Full screen mode. The vocabulary and software totality examples support the composition without constituting a whole-caption attestation. [Shull, p.8](https://conference.usu.edu/SYSTEM/Uploads/Handouts/15233_1981miriamredleaf.pdf#page=8) · [Android totality example](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/apps/Settings/+/7c598253ff60f06f8e6fe046f18fd88e9daa72d3/res/values-am/strings.xml#2722)
- **አሳይ** is a supported show imperative, following the object and intervening adverbial phrases. It makes the instruction display-facing instead of directing a change to the canvas's stored dimensions. [Android show command](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/apps/Settings/+/7c598253ff60f06f8e6fe046f18fd88e9daa72d3/res/values-am/strings.xml#5109)

The desktop-window-to-editor-viewport transfer is accepted as a contextual judgment at **medium confidence**. Here the canvas is the object and the window is the display target, so the construction keeps the two roles distinct. Combined with entirety and show, the conventional window-fit idiom communicates fitting the canvas into the available display area, rather than stretching it, changing the application window, or filling the device screen. It need not assert exact edge-to-edge occupancy; padding, aspect-ratio preservation and other editing bounds are compatible with fitting in view.

This is not a claim that the wording is the uniquely best idiom or that every reader will interpret it identically. Nevertheless, there is no identified grammatical or semantic defect that warrants retaining the old translation-precision hold. Requiring an exact pre-existing full caption would incorrectly prevent an otherwise supported new composition. The separate short Fit label's historical acceptance is consistency context only and was not used as authority for this decision.

Fit uses the full bitmap rectangle plus outlying trim and transformed floating-selection bounds. It computes a uniform, aspect-preserving scale against both drawable viewport dimensions, reserves padding, and centers that union. The viewport excludes scrollbars and optional rulers. It changes zoom, pan and fit state, and resets polygon-tap bookkeeping; a very small view can return early. It does not resize or crop the editable document or perform the separate selection-fitting operation. [Fit and bounds](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L160-L192) · [Rotated selection bounds](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/SelectionGeometry.kt#L8-L25)

Drawing applies viewport clipping, translation and uniform scaling. A reduced display bitmap may be produced for an overview. That rendering resampling does not replace or destructively resize the editable document, so the defensible claim is **no destructive document resample**, not **no resampling anywhere**. [Drawing transform](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L237-L287) · [Display-only overview](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/CanvasBitmapOverview.kt#L9-L44)

No English-source defect has been established. Including additional editing bounds does not contradict fitting the entire canvas. If canonical English later changes, flag and review the affected term across languages rather than repair its meaning only in Amharic.
