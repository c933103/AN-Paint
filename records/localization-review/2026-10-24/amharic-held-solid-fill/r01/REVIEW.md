# Independent adjudication: Amharic Solid fill

## Decision

**RELEASE WITH REVISION.** Accept **የነጠላ ቀለም መሙያ** for **string/ui_solid_fill** (English: **Solid fill**). Its contextual gloss is **single-colour fill**. Replace the held **መሙያ ብቻ**; do not retain it as an alternative accepted value.

This resolves the translation fidelity hold only. It does not implement the string, authorize an application release, or certify native-reader, runtime, visual-fit or accessibility acceptance. The supplied review inventory would move from 100 ready / 8 held to **101 ready / 7 held** when this decision is recorded. Implemented remains **0** and the pending **90** are untouched. No external inventory was edited by this review.

## Why the revision passes

The [earlier hold](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/fe51d699eed5b068444c46168339b41b6cd1010a/records/localization-review/2026-10-24/amharic-brush-shape/r01/independent/REVIEW.md) was justified: “fill only” can describe a patterned fill without an outline and therefore drops the source's uniform-colour meaning. The new caption repairs that specific omission rather than merely describing the absence of a stroke.

The [English graphics definition](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/powerpoint.fillformat.solid) identifies solid fill as uniform colour. In this shape-style setting, **ነጠላ ቀለም** specifies a single colour and **መሙያ** names the fill. Both components have relevant primary software usage: [one colour assigned per polygon](https://github.com/LibreOffice/translations/blob/681a7f1e79cd6e27496259dab7940c8e490059de/source/am/svx/messages.po#L15414-L15418) and the [graphics Fill label](https://github.com/LibreOffice/translations/blob/681a7f1e79cd6e27496259dab7940c8e490059de/source/am/svx/messages.po#L19823-L19827). These records are one localization authority, not independent votes; their nonfuzzy state is not a quality guarantee. The surrounding LibreOffice prose is not adopted as a model sentence.

The whole caption is a productive composition, not a claimed quotation. Its grouping is **[የ [ነጠላ ቀለም]] [መሙያ]**: the single-colour qualifier precedes the fill head, with የ- at the qualifier's beginning. [Leslau's construction discussion](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/pluginfile.php/6774/mod_resource/content/0/amharic_rg.pdf#page=66) supports the prefix placement before an adjective within the qualifying noun phrase, and permits relations beyond literal ownership. [Adafre's independent grammatical analysis](https://aclanthology.org/2004.eamt-1.6.pdf#page=3) corroborates prenominal modifiers and yä-marked nominal expressions. Applying these rules to this graphics caption is this review's compositional judgment, not either author's endorsement of it.

I find the resulting noun phrase intelligible and grammatically well formed for the three-option control. It does not merely mean complete coverage, an opaque object, background fill or removal of an outline. “Single colour” is read here as the property of the fill, not as a monochrome image transformation allowing tonal variation. The actual uniform-colour operation and the adjacent **Outline / Fill + line** options support that reading. It need not add “foreground” or “without outline,” which the English label also leaves implicit. No concrete remaining semantic or grammatical defect justifies continuing this hold. An exact previous full-caption match is neither claimed nor required.

## Source meaning and the controlling opacity correction

The [unchanged English resource](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/res/values/strings.xml#L379-L382) is the middle item in the [three-choice style control](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ClassicPaintActivity.kt#L785-L800). For fill-capable shapes, the [rendering branch](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L272-L286) gives style 0 a foreground stroke only; style 1 a uniform foreground fill with no separate stroke pass; and style 2 a background fill plus a foreground stroke. No gradient, pattern or texture is introduced in this inspected branch. This is shape styling, not flood fill.

The correction is material and controlling. The [foreground and background setters](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L44-L47) OR every assignment with [Color.BLACK, 0xff000000](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/graphics/Color#BLACK), preserving RGB while forcing stored alpha to 255. The [sampled pixel](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L811-L813), [ordinary colour selection](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ClassicPaintActivity.kt#L1425-L1427) and [dialog preview](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/ClassicPaintActivity.kt#L954-L958) all pass through these setters. The [paint setup](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintDocument.kt#L247-L258) and shape-fill assignment use the stored colour; [Paint.setColor(int)](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/graphics/Paint#setColor(int)) includes its alpha. The relevant shape choices are not Watercolor, so its separate alpha adjustment does not qualify their fill behaviour. Thus current style 1 paints an **opaque foreground** fill, and style 2 uses **opaque background** fill and foreground stroke.

Antialiased boundary coverage may blend edge pixels independently of the selected colour's or fill paint's opacity. Uniform fill is not a claim that every final boundary pixel is identical. [Transparency in other software](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/powerpoint.fillformat.transparency) only demonstrates that the general English graphics term is not synonymous with opacity; it does not give AN Paint a transparency capability. The accepted caption makes no transparency claim. Replacing “solid” with “opaque” would still fail to express uniform colour.

**Open Polygon remains unfilled.** Both [preview](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L272-L277) and [commit](https://github.com/c933103/AN-Paint/blob/66be61df27c5359bffc32d56db21f3949899f873/Paintroid/src/main/java/org/catrobat/paintroid/classic/PaintCanvas.kt#L443-L446) dispatch it as LINE; the drawShape branch always strokes LINE/CURVE and excludes them from interior fill, regardless of the selected style. The caption is not a promise to fill an open polygon. No independently justified English-source defect was established, so no source change or affected-languages source-change pass is triggered here.

It still does not resolve the full/complete ambiguity of this alternative.

No demonstrated defect requires replacing the selected yä construction.
- **ወጥ መሙያ:** not selected without sufficiently specific evidence in this record.

Source interpretation: **high**. Translation fidelity and lexical composition: **medium-high**. Full-caption idiom/UI preference: **medium**. This is an evidence-based translation-review judgment, not native certification. The remaining uncertainty concerns stylistic preference and future UI evaluation, not an identified failure to translate “solid.”
