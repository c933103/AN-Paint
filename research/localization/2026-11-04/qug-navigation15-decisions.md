# Final P1 controls review

Result: retain 15 proposed additions and all four dependent edits. Hold ui_main23 (Hatun) for actual caller context. No replacement word is sufficiently justified for this context-free Main label. No original draft or repository changes made.

## Terms

Airbrush: retain Wayrawan tullpuna as an authored air-painting paraphrase for the spray tool. It does not need an invented or unsupported spray root.

Ellipse: retain Elipse as an explicitly disclosed Spanish mathematical loan, not as a claimed indigenous attestation or unnoticed English fallback. It is more precise than replacing ellipse with a possibly broader oval description.

Polygon: retain Achka manyayuk rikchay. Many-sided shape is a reasonable contextual description, and does not wrongly promise a regular polygon.

Watercolor: retain Yaku tullpu for the simulated brush. No need to hold merely because a conventional regional technical term was not established.

Main: Hatun can express great/major, but its broad large sense is a genuine risk when the noun and UI placement are unknown. Caller context is needed; do not substitute Home/Initial on speculation.

Fit image: retain the generic Shuyuta paktachina. It makes no unsupported viewport, canvas-size, or resampling claim. Missing caller limits behavioral verification, but does not itself make this generic wording wrong.
