# Cherokee and Zhuang source attribution

Current canonical additions: four Cherokee filename-sort/quality/largest labels and eighteen Zhuang gallery, assembly, memory and colour controls. Bounded whole-value source/context review, not native-speaker or whole-language certification. Ten rejected/deferred proposals are excluded.

Zhuang terminology reference: Sawroeg, https://github.com/libertas/sawroeg/tree/a1efe4a78eb1e88722e6cee01b95ad21c259275d, `dbs/sawguq.db` and `tools/sawguq.txt`. Authors credited upstream: Daegroeg (Libertas), Bya'mbin (Blambin), Sam Bliss (StarBrilliant). GPL version 3; original COPYING retained as `source-supported-licences/sawroeg-GPL-3.0.txt`. Bouyei selgus.db was not used. New complete clauses are adaptations, not claims of verbatim source sentences.

Cherokee filename phrase reuses existing canonical save20_file_name in the identical filename context, with invariant A–Z/Z–A sort markers. Generic quality/largest lexical labels are referenced to Cherokee Nation OU word list, https://language.cherokee.gov/media/ykahxw4v/oudictionaryeuglutan.pdf, pages 572 and 402. This word list is not evidence certifying complete grammar or inflections.
