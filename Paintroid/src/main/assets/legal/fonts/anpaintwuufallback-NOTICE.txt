AN Paint Wu Fallback (modified glyph-only subset), AN Paint contributors, 2026.
Source: Nom Na Tong v5.18, Vietnamese Nôm Preservation Foundation / Nôm Na Group.
https://github.com/nomfoundation/font/releases/tag/v5.18
Original TTF SHA256: 37bd8506257d0905d499395e7f0bc2b6a2ae619dbb635a82ea0905b75b2ff5f3

Modified: subset to the Wu catalogue's supplementary character U+20C8E (𠲎),
removed unused layout tables, renamed family. Other Wu characters use the
Android/WebView system fallback, preserving their usual glyph forms.
This internal UI fallback is not offered as a complete drawing font.
Rebuild with tools/build_nom_ui_font.py and the pinned upstream fonts.
