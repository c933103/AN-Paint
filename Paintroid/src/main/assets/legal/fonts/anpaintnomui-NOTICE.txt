AN Paint Nom UI (modified subset), AN Paint contributors, 2026.
A renamed font combining a subset of Nom Na Tong v5.18 (MIT) with missing
Nôm glyphs from Gothic Nguyen (SIL OFL 1.1, derived from Source Han Sans).
The combined modified font is distributed under SIL OFL 1.1, and retains
the original MIT notice for Nom Na Tong below.

Gothic Nguyen, MORI Takayuki:

Original Git blob SHA1: 7edfe73d9b730e3ae3422fd5d8c7bd73b8b9ac18

Modified: subset to catalogue/picker glyphs, merged disjoint glyph ranges,
removed layout tables unused by this horizontal UI font, renamed family.
Rebuild with tools/build_nom_ui_font.py and the two pinned source fonts.
