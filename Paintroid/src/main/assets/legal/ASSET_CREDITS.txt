DYNAMIC WORKSPACE CONTROLS
The FG/BG status display, colour spectrum/wheel (AdvancedColourDialog.kt),
hexagonal swatches (HoneycombPalette.kt), selection outlines and transform
handles (PaintCanvas.kt / SelectionGeometry.kt), the 100% zoom tick (ZoomSeekBar.kt),
touch crop handles (CropOverlay.kt), thumbnail framing and snap-preview boundaries show current
application or image state. Their drawing code remains AGPL-3.0-or-later.
Assembly thumbnails display the user's imported images. Android supplies the
landscape submenu indicators; its existing notices apply.
