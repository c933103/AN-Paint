Nine additional choices use Android system families (sans-serif, serif,
monospace and variants). These and missing-glyph fallback fonts are supplied by
the device, whose open-source notices identify their files and copyright holders.
No Dubai or STC/GE SS font files are included. Inspection of the exact upstream
files identifies Dubai Regular 1.10 and Boutros GE SS Text Light 1.200; the latter was labelled STC upstream, a name whose relationship to the internal
family identity is not explained in the reviewed records. Dubai's binary contains a limited-use licence notice;
the separately published Dubai EULA also excludes image-generating apps.
Boutros's public terms restrict copying and distribution without an additional
agreement. We have not established a sufficient redistribution grant for AN Paint.
Upstream added them for Arabic text; the public introducing PRs do not establish
what permission Catrobat relied on. Their inclusion is not proof either way of
upstream authorization. Full evidence, sources
and the Dubai EULA mirror's provenance are in FONT-LICENCE-REVIEW.md and
verification/legacy-font-metadata.json in the exported source.
The former legacy selector used system-font substitutes; it has been deleted.
