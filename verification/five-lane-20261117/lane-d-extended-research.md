# Lane D extended source pass

The four initial accepted labels are not a stopping criterion. A second pass followed entire missing-key inventories, short-label candidates, exact and punctuation-normalized donor matching, lexical dictionary senses, bounded composition and additional upstream UI repositories.

## New candidate evidence

Tai Nüa: Luo (1999) printed p.30/PDF70 explicitly lists honeycomb under hoŋ²; p.152/PDF192 explicitly gives largest yai³ si¹ pən⁶ and the productive superlative marker; p.41/PDF81 gives top/on top/above kan⁴ lə¹. Smallest is a proposed composition of small/tiny (?ɛt¹, p.6/PDF46) with the attested superlative marker, and must be evaluated as composition rather than an exact phrase quotation. Historical marks were visually inspected and mapped through Unicode L2/2024/24098. Candidate and review status are separate from canonical acceptance.

## Negative results and rejected shortcuts

- Punctuation-normalized exact English matching across pinned MediaWiki nv/tdd and all remaining gaps found no further new exact donor pair beyond the initial Navajo Search label. This does not prove no usable translation exists elsewhere.
- Fresh GitHub reads of wikimedia/oojs-ui i18n/nv.json, wikimedia/VisualEditor i18n/nv.json and wikimedia/mediawiki-extensions-VisualEditor i18n/ve-mw/nv.json at master returned 404. Tai Nüa UploadWizard i18n/tdd.json also returned 404. These are path-level observations, not claims that an entire language ecosystem is absent.
- WikimediaMessages Tai Nüa wikimedia/tdd.json exists. Its legal terms describe Wikimedia website publishing and licensing; transplanting them would misstate AN Paint's software, font and image obligations, so no legal paragraphs were copied.
- Wall and Morgan (1958), printed p.60/PDF67, visually checked wagon-wheel entry: specifically tsinaabąąs bijáád. A vehicle-specific compound is not accepted as a generic colour-wheel translation. Steering-wheel/rudder senses also fail the UI context test.
- OJB-Quantum/Navaho-Linguistics README at a2a0a2944e6cdb337bf1fc2abbe7269a13ac7bfb was rescanned for measurement, shape, size, colour and computing terms. Small-piece, same-amount and refrigerator-size phrases do not directly establish smallest/largest image dimension, arbitrary width/height placeholders or editor instructions. No quantum-computing sentences were recomposed into UI prose.
- Search result ED440481 was opened and excluded: it is a general transition curriculum, not a Navajo bilingual mathematics glossary. Search snippets alone were not used as translation evidence.

Remaining blocker: the majority of both inventories are domain-specific multi-clause instructions, runtime errors and legal disclosures. Their acceptance needs reliable phrase-level language evidence or qualified language review. Dictionary roots and mechanical Shan-to-Tai-Le conversion do not provide that. No external translator was contacted, hired or claimed to have certified the work.
