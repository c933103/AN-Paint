## Independent findings and corrections

The integration review read the complete source/proposed-value comparison and checked 477 distinct production consumer references against the pinned source. The source and the three starting catalogues are unchanged from the author's `4a5c671ad1003197e3836f1fbdc41ab8c458ffd6` base. Specific corrections were incorporated before acceptance:

- Raw-recovery action/title/failure labels and the active paused-autosave path no longer assert that copied bytes are a valid ZIP. New warnings retain possible damage/unreadability, both ZIP-opening and readable-canvas conditions, unverified ancillary contents, and retention even if unopenable. Obsolete source-only legacy explanations retain their source meaning; quoted controls were rebound.
- Animation uncertainty distinguishes inability to establish **one versus multiple frames** from ignorance of an exact count. Poster, first-frame and still-export distinctions remain.
- Central/Western assembly source, saved/stored work and exported PNG are explicit rather than describing an undifferentiated image. Save and derivative exports remain separate.
- Pinch-in/out pairs with smaller/larger in the correct order. Fit-selection means containment, not fill/cover. Crop, image resizing, canvas bounds and viewport zoom stay distinct; source preservation and memory-versus-storage distinctions remain.
- Geometry and ratio captions include functional descriptions and explicit borrowed technical identifiers. Rectangle has four 90-degree corners; polygon does not falsely require closed paths; ellipse includes circles. Percent is a per-hundred ratio, not an upper limit of 100.
- Central `sempa` repetition usage was independently supported by a primary Central publication. Existing `Naja xijchiua` was retained against Hidalguense again-versus-I evidence. Eastern paint and same/similar senses were checked in modern IDIEZ evidence, not rejected using unrelated historical glosses. No mechanical cross-variety spelling normalization was applied.

No concrete unresolved meaning error remains in these 598 changes under this bounded source-informed review. This is not native-speaker endorsement. Regional evidence is strongest for the respective source varieties; it does not attest every complete authored software sentence, particularly long Western technical compositions. Existing eraser-agent wording retains a lexical-confidence caveat. Spanish identifiers such as elipse, polígono, rectángulo, por ciento and pincel are explicit editorial borrowings, not alleged standardized regional terminology.

## Evidence and reuse

Primary/regional sources include the [Hidalguense author's vocabulary page](https://mhernandez95.wordpress.com/2009/02/01/vocabulario-nahuatl-espanol-de-la-huasteca-hidalguense/), its explicitly secondary [full-text transcription](https://nahuatlhuasteca.wordpress.com/2012/12/27/el-diccionario/), the [modern Chicontepec/IDIEZ dictionary](https://www.academia.edu/34987634/Tlahtolxitlauhcayotl_Chicontepec_Veracruz), and modern IDIEZ fields in the University of Oregon entries for [drag](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/totoxo%C4%81) and [pull](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/tilana). Historical and modern entries were distinguished. The full author-hosted Hidalguense PDF was not obtained; the transcription's limitations were retained.

Western evidence includes [INALI's Potosina glossary](https://site.inali.gob.mx/bicen/pdf/GLOSARIO_nahuatl_huasteca_potosina.pdf) and its [actual language-chooser UI](https://constitucionenlenguas.inali.gob.mx/nahuatl-de-la-huasteca/articulos/001). Primary Central repetition evidence is [La Liga Bíblica Internacional's 2004 Central publication](https://www.scriptureearth.org/data/nch/PDF/09-1Sa-nch-web.pdf), printed 390–391 / PDF 4–5, repeated-call context. These support lexical senses; no dictionary or religious sentences were copied into application text. Unavailable/403 sources were not credited or bypassed.

The stable temporary author manifest SHA-256 is `a026a8239345c045d82967ee0dfb0b12572b6d167672b90ce8b71a4080392a3f`. Canonical XML remains the wording authority; no permanent full-catalogue approval mirror is added. Existing valid earlier reviews remain usable when the value, source and relevant consumer match. This inventory neither withdraws prior acceptance nor counts repeated review as new work. Future review targets unreviewed/changed entries and coverage-lineage gaps, not blanket re-reading of unchanged accepted strings.
