## Added scope and independent review

**P0 assembly minimum-output warning:** `ui_assembly_source_memory_floor`. The warning covers the complete estimate, including resident images and output/edit buffers, not merely the decoder. Even a 1×1 output can fail. The advice preserves closing other documents or preparing smaller input images, retrying, and the assembly remaining unchanged at this early return. “Current AN Paint RAM allowance” is not total installed RAM, disk space or a promise that any smaller output succeeds.

**Seven P1 import/memory entries:** `ui_no_resize_fits_decoder_memory`, `ui_codec_resize_memory_floor`, `ui_resize_the_copy`, `ui_the_decoder_and_editing_buffers_need_about_the`, `ui_the_full_image_and_working_buffers_exceed_the`, `ui_too_large_for_the_current_budget_reduce_the`, `ui_choose_a_smaller_output_copy_the_source_images`.

These distinguish native full-image/tile work from the editable output, memory-allocation failure from budget rejection, a separately made resized image from modifying the original, and output dimensions from original-coordinate crop edges. Review replaced an ambiguous smaller “original” instruction with smaller input for opening and used the allocation-specific `faenboiq`. Size-dialog confirmation rereads current policy; rejection and Cancel are not successful import. Buffer and tile language is functional (“data used to open/edit”, “parts of the image”), not a claimed standardized term.

**Nineteen P1 color/control entries:** `ui_basic_colours`, `ui_bg`, `ui_fg`, `ui_edit_background`, `ui_edit_foreground`, `ui_reset_to_black_white`, `ui_swap_foreground_background`, `ui_palette`, `ui_expand_colour_palette`, `ui_collapse_colour_palette`, `ui_foreground_background_colour_palette`, `ui_colour_palette_tap_for_foreground_hold_for_background`, `ui_hide_editor_controls`, `ui_show_editor_controls`, `ui_show_controls`, `ui_tool_categories`, `ui_expand_tool_category`, `ui_collapse_tool_category`, `ui_left_arrow_collapse`.

Eight have direct production consumers: basic colors, FG/BG captions, palette mode, palette gestures, show controls, and category expand/collapse. The other eleven are retained legacy/source-only entries; they get lexical/format review, not runtime credit. Basic colors refers to the 48-swatch section, not three RGB primaries. Tap sets foreground, long press sets background, horizontal scrolling reveals colors. Category descriptions name the category and remembered selected tool before the expand/collapse action; opening another category can activate its remembered tool. Hide/show is distinguished from shrink/unfold. The inactive palette summary preserves `%1$s`/`%2$s` color roles and `%3$s` as a separate supplied palette state; its state supplier must be checked if reactivated.

**Five P1 selection entries:** `ui_cut`, `ui_paste`, `ui_free_form_select`, `ui_outline`, `ui_invalid_selection_outline`. Cut/Paste use supported physical-action words contextually. Cut first copies the transformed selection, then deletes it; Paste creates a floating selection from the app bitmap clipboard, not system text or a saved file. Free-form select describes drawing the area boundary. Outline labels the stroked, unfilled shape style. The invalid-outline message says the selection boundary cannot be used: its explicit consumer rejects more than 128 recovered contours, rather than accusing the current lasso drawing or entire bitmap of corruption.
