## Independently checked primary grounds

- [C–V halda](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0233.html): continuation fram + dative activity; no requirement to preserve modern áfram.
- [C–V deila](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0098.html): accusative division/distribution object; digital sharing stays new.
- [B–T oþ-stillan](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oe_bosworthtoller/b0770.html) and [hwílend-líc](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oe_bosworthtoller/b0576.html): stop participle and temporary adjective grounded. The adverb/whole UI clause is a productive new use, not directly attested quotation.
- [C–V hlutr/hlutfelling](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0273.html): chosen common strong pixel-head paradigm and historical proportion noun. Weak hluti for portion is independently legitimate.
- [B–T ærra/ærre](https://bosworthtoller.com/731), [ge-metan](https://bosworthtoller.com/15222), [écan](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-E) and [freó](https://bosworthtoller.com/46387): specific form choices, not whole-sentence certification.
- [B–T sméþian](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-S): singular weak-II -aþ exists. Reviewer withdrew earlier overbroad agreement criticism, explicitly amended in batch3 report.
- [B–T prica](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-P) supports pricena. [UT weak nouns](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/eieol/engol/80) also permits reduced genitive -a; this is normalization, not a proven mandatory repair.
- [B–T arædan](https://bosworthtoller.com/38876), [bisen](https://bosworthtoller.com/40422), [gearu](https://bosworthtoller.com/13504): actual lexical bases inspected. Historical spelling/gender alternatives are acknowledged; selected new compound paradigms are editorial conventions.

## Remaining high-impact gates

1. Recovery-copy/draft/restoration and undo terminology must be intelligible and consistent, not merely historically shaped words.
2. Sampled decoding must not collapse into generic part-wise/tiled decoding; resolution must not silently become scalar pixel count; colour profile/model/metadata must remain distinct.
3. Variable tool labels include nouns of different gender and infinitival actions. The current selected-status construction requires separate revision/context review; see batch5 amendment. No automatic acceptance based on neutral valit/gecoren.
4. Lexical uncertainties remain distinct from implementation approval. Read-only source context checks are documented separately; no application changes or project test results are asserted.
