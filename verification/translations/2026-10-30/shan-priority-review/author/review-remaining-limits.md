# Remaining item-specific Shan semantic limits

`remaining-semantic-holds.tsv` records the **21 final directly missing items (15 P0 + 6 P1)** not offered as complete candidates. These are linguistic/semantic limits after checking actual source meanings, not permission denials or an assertion that Shan cannot express the concepts.

Every row identifies the specific unsafe substitution and the source propositions that still need a dependable full target. Several clauses are individually expressible; they are not passed off as complete fluent translations while their central noun/action remains insecure.

Evidence-informed distinctions:

- `ImageAssembly` stores original source files and reversible crop/position/attachment metadata. Seven assembly-dependent messages are each held for the structured object, not casually flattened to ordinary output image. Their separate failure/preservation/budget meanings remain documented.
- `ImageCredit` retains original attribution source/text independently of the UI language. Three credit-dependent messages concern durable attribution records and recoverability, not financial credit or merely a visible caption. Licence/source/creator possibilities make a creator-name-only paraphrase too narrow.
- The **slash** part of the filename message is now directly supported by [Unicode CLDR's Shan summary](https://unicode.org/cldr/charts/49/summary/shn.html), punctuation slash row 8104 and backslash row 8106. The revised whole sentence remains held because its control-character compound is not confidently established. This is progress on one dependency, not a false acceptance.
- Metadata/converter wording was drafted elsewhere using [primary MediaWiki Shan metadata terminology](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wikimedia/mediawiki/master/languages/i18n/shn.json). That does not establish ICC model/profile or colour-managed application, which remain distinct concepts here.
- `PaintDocument.invert` complements RGB while preserving alpha. A general convert-colours or swap-colours label would be wrong. `ScanlineFill` tolerance and pan-only Navigate were drafted separately rather than blocked with inversion.
- The exact PNG default-image distinction, PDF vector-to-pixel conversion, GIF dithering/gradient relation, ellipse geometry, coordinate range and touchscreen pinch are recorded per row. Neither English key-name inference nor absent search results is used as semantic evidence.
