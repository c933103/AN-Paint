## Evidence and narrow qualifications

- [UT OE weak nouns §36](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/eieol/engol/80) explicitly permits reduced genitive plural -a. [B–T prica](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-P) directly evidences pricena; selecting -pricena is well-supported normalization, not proof that every -prica is impossible.
- [B–T cynn](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-C) gives plural cynn in Ælfric. Proposed compound should follow this chosen normalized paradigm or document a justified alternate.
- [C–V fingr](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0152.html) gives masculine plural fingr: do not replace tvá fingr just because modern Icelandic differs.
- [C–V kostr](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/k.html) supports at minnsta kosti; modern-looking phrase is not itself proof of anachronism.
- [B–T sméþian](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-S) demonstrates weak-II singular -aþ. No general plural-only rule is applied here.
