## Primary checks and limited claims

- [C–V halda, p.233](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0233.html): modern áfram versus historical fram continuation.
- [C–V hlutr, p.273](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0273.html): common strong plural; older alternatives are not globally prohibited, but the draft must follow its declared paradigm.
- [B–T ge-metan](https://bosworthtoller.com/15222): weak painting participle -mett/-met; not gemetod.
- [B–T ge-þwærian](https://bosworthtoller.com/16533): accord/mildness, not complexity.
- [C–V einn](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/e.html): historical at eins for only.
- [C–V deila, p.98](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0098.html): accusative object in divide/deal sense. No claim that digital sharing is medieval.
- [B–T forlēosan, p.314](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oe_bosworthtoller/b0314.html): reviewed examples do not establish the draft's genitive object; absence here is not a universal proof.
- [C–V eptir-rit, p.131](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0131.html): marked modern; all instances must keep explicit reconstructed/modern status.

Remaining HOLDs require linguistic/terminology evidence. This review does not authorize source integration.
