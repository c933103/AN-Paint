## Evidence-backed corrections

- **ang `Ærra weorcgewrit` → `Ærre weorcgewrit`:** the noun is declared neuter; the comparative is masculine `ærra`, feminine/neuter `ærre`. [B–T ǽr](https://bosworthtoller.com/731).
- **non strong pixel paradigm:** the declared noun is `myndhlutr`, with strong `hlutr` as head. Use nominative plural `myndhlutir`, accusative plural `myndhluti`, genitive plural `myndhluta`, dative plural `myndhlutum`. Thus the two batch-2 accusative `myndhluta` values need correction. The independently available weak noun `hluti` must not be silently conflated with this chosen strong lemma. [C–V hlutr, p.273](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0273.html).
- **ang autosave `abiden`:** this comes from a verb of remaining/waiting/awaiting, not a securely established transitive “pause” verb; a passive announcement of suspended automatic saving needs a different construction. Examine an explicitly temporary stopping formulation such as `hwilendlice gestilled`, without treating that proposed phrase as already accepted. [B–T ā-bīdan, p.3](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-A).
- **ang make-too-large:** in `don þa metinge to micelre`, `micelre` is dative/genitive feminine. Here “too” is an adverb and the adjective should agree with the accusative object; examine `don þa metinge to micle`. [UT adjective paradigms](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/eieol/engol/90).
- **No blanket rejection of progressive `vera at`:** historical `vera at` plus infinitive is documented. The narrower unresolved question is the exact impersonal passive-like `Nú er verit at varðveita ...`; evidence for the former does not automatically validate the latter. An explicit present-tense AN Paint subject would avoid that problem. [Zoëga, vera](https://norse.ulver.com/dct/zoega/v.html).
- **stöðva is not inherently modern:** an edited historical-text glossary supplies the verb. The issue in the paused row is temporary suspension versus unqualified stopping, not an invented claim that the verb never existed. [Viking Society Hávamál glossary](https://vsnr.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Havamal-complete.pdf).
