## Evidence and limits

- [B–T écan/eácan](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-E): supports transitive increase/add versus strong intransitive entry; does not itself certify a software Add label.
- [B–T freó](https://bosworthtoller.com/46387): attested datival forms support agreement objection to mid frige hiwe.
- [C–V auka](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0033.html): thing added may be dative; do not falsely reject riti/lit solely for being dative.
- [C–V hlutr](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0273.html): common plural and historical hlutfelling versus modern proportion sense hlutfall. Modern wording is not forbidden if honestly a chosen neologism, but v1 did not adequately distinguish that status.
- [C–V deila](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0098.html): accusative in divide/deal sense.
- [B–T hiw](https://bosworthtoller.com/19127): colour sense grounded; exact hiwu plural unresolved, not asserted impossible.
- [UT OE adjectives](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/eieol/engol/90): feminine/neuter plural -e variants exist; no blanket normalization to -a imposed.
