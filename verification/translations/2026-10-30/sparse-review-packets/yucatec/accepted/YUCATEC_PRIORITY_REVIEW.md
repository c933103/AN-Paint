## Semantic and structural findings

- **Save:** the new lexical family has direct Yucatec software Save/image-saving evidence. Independent digital-media prose corroborates active, passive and result-state morphology. Recording usage alone would not prove file Save. The old Líikʼs proposals remain uncertain, not categorically disproven by a raise/lift dictionary gloss.
- **Status and sharing:** the saved placeholder names the actual output file; the label does not guarantee every current editor change is saved. Share names the user action/chooser, not already-completed transmission. Both save and share remain expressed in their coordinated labels.
- **Dimensions:** positivity excludes zero; no-larger-than-original permits equality. Resize changes image size, not view magnification. Original size refers to restoration of the selection's source dimensions. Valid dimensions are operationally usable, not morally good or assumed to satisfy a narrower invented bound.
- **Tool/typography labels:** height is a noun rather than high/above; eraser is an instrument, not a working draft; underline explicitly places a line beneath text, not subscript; bold describes weight, not bravery.
- **Invariants:** source placeholder names, positions and types are preserved, including error-detail placement and lower/upper-bound order. JPEG XL is not reduced to JPEG. PNG, #RRGGBB, 50–300%, zero and chooser ellipses are retained. Integration must preserve `formatted=false` on `ui_use_50_300`, `ui_original_size34` and invariant `ui_100`.

Per-item **medium-high** confidence denotes direct lexical/morphological support plus bounded contextual use. **Medium** denotes a defensible authored composition or technical extension; it does not claim a published exact whole sentence. Exact full-sentence precedent was not required for ordinary composition. Conversely, short lexical evidence was not used to approve unresolved recovery, destination, memory, codec, credit-retention or animation-loss warnings.

Mozilla data was checked for explicit `yua` language tagging and exact source context. Its heterogeneous and sometimes mixed-Spanish content is not blanket linguistic authority. Repeated current/legacy entries are corroborating usage, not necessarily independent translator judgments. No long upstream warning was imported.
