## Why the convention is adequate

The author now defines a functional class of structured descriptive/control records, textual or binary, whether embedded or archive-associated. It explicitly excludes an inference of prose, necessarily external or appended storage, optionality, disposability or raw pixel content. That addresses the precise ambiguity previously left unresolved. Neither target has been shown inherently to require the excluded readings.

The compounds remain new technical vocabulary. B–T establishes the ordinary side noun and the neuter written-record base; C–V supplies addition and the neuter writing noun. These support a disclosed formation and inflection, **not** historical binary metadata or a pre-existing software prefix. Acceptance is an inference about a deliberately defined modern extension. Unaided reader comprehension has not been measured, which keeps confidence moderate. [B–T síde](https://bosworthtoller.com/27617), [B–T ge-writ](https://bosworthtoller.com/16905), [C–V BÆTA / við-bót](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/b.html), [C–V RIT](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/r.html).

## Separate morphology and clause findings

### ANG short invalid-state adjective

`Unrihte` need not be normalized to `Unriht` merely from an elementary zero-ending paradigm. Moore and Knott explicitly give both forms for neuter nominative/accusative plural adjectives and explain analogical `-e`. This is evidence about the neuter, not an irrelevant appeal to feminine plural endings. It supports retaining the short caption while `unriht` in the longer colour clause also remains possible. [Moore and Knott, *The Elements of Old English*, §353, printed p.156 / PDF p.170](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a6/The_elements_of_Old_English%3B_elementary_grammar_and_reference_grammar_%28IA_elementsofoldeng00mooriala%29.pdf).

### ANG colour truncation: the whole clause can now be accepted

B–T's supplement explicitly supplies intransitive `endian` and the example **“Hér endaþ seó æftre fróferbóc”**. Thus the written-object subject with singular `endaþ` has positive evidence; this is not an invented agreement correction. `Innan` has genitive constructions, matching `his hiwsidegewrita`. The new metaphor maps a file's termination to a position within its encoded record span; it does not assert that metadata is a separate external object. [B–T supplement, endian, p.190](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oe_bosworthtoller/d0190.html), [B–T innan](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-I).

### ANG invalid/unsupported: retain the narrower HOLD

B–T documents receive/accept senses and genitive, dative and accusative objects for `on-fón`. The issue is therefore **not** an invented genitive-only rule. `Onfo` can be read as subjunctive; its generic/modal force in this exact relative clause has not been sufficiently established here as the desired factual unsupported-state statement. An indicative alternative must not be imposed merely because a subjunctive exists. The value remains HOLD rather than CORRECT; metadata approval alone does not decide this finite-clause choice. [B–T on-fón](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/asd/dict-O).

### NON invalid/unsupported: accept separately from the glossary

The receipt/acceptance use of `taka við` has historical support. The clause's indeclinable relative `er` does not require the matrix antecedent `slík` to be changed to the dative: the antecedent is an alternative object of `hefir`, while the relative construction supplies the received item. `Ógild` / `slík` are coherent neuter plural forms; `um liti` keeps colour scope. The accepted earlier supported-image clause uses the same receiving construction, so no unresolved base-idiom dependency is being hidden. The software-support extension remains explicitly modern. [*Egils saga*, glossary, taka við](https://vsnr.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Egils_saga.pdf).

### NON truncation: a specific historical construction remains open

C–V's ENDA entry distinguishes the reflexive come-to-an-end construction, historical impersonal **`sögu endar`**, and modern nominative-subject **`saga endar`**. That does not prove the current sentence impossible: neuter `Myndritit` has the same nominative and accusative surface, leaving an impersonal analysis available in principle. But such an analysis and the `sinna` reference have not been independently settled. The author intended a file-subject clause; replacing this question with unexamined modern-Icelandic intransitive usage would be inappropriate. **HOLD, not CORRECT.** The attested middle `endisk` may be a useful direction for a separately reviewed future proposal, but no replacement is accepted by this delta. [C–V ENDA II–III](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/e.html).

`Innan` with genitive itself has historical support; its application to serialized record boundaries is a disclosed modern metaphor, not grounds for a blanket hold. [C–V innan](https://norroen.info/dct/cleasby/i.html).
