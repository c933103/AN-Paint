## Sources and limits

The [Chamorro community dictionary](https://natibunmarianas.org/chamorro-dictionary/) directly supports paintbrush, canvas, blurred, edge, equipment/device, from and an underline example. Full software sentences are authored compositions. The [Ecuador Ministry Kichwa dictionary](https://www.kichwa.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/diccionario_kichwa_castellano.pdf) supports principal/important and arrow senses. These are not universal Quechua terminology claims.

The [UQROO Maya dictionary](https://www.uqroo.mx/libros/maya/diccionario.pdf) supports uncertainty, damaged, examine/test and change. The independent reviewer visually inspected PDF 72 / printed 71 of the [teaching text](https://www.uqroo.mx/libros/maya/maya3.pdf), confirming perhaps and although-not constructions. The ability-to-resize modal is not used to imply healthy recovery contents. Current [Mozilla Focus Yucatec resources](https://github.com/mozilla-firefox/firefox/blob/main/mobile/android/focus-android/app/src/main/res/values-yua/strings.xml) corroborate Settings/Save senses, not every inflection or whole sentence.

Existing Kichwa height and rectangle terminology retain regional-evidence limits: indexed Ecuadorian height evidence was found but its primary page was not inspected; the rectangle compound has Pastaza mirror support, not exact Chimborazo certification. These are named follow-ups, not an excuse to invent replacements. Existing Yucatec pixel labels are neutral `px` abbreviations, not expanded lexical or spoken-accessibility completion. Technical loans, numerical templates and source-only keys remain separately classified. No native fluency, mathematical-term standardization, device layout or TalkBack acceptance is claimed. Long labels and formula speech need actual runtime checks.
