## Review coverage and findings

### English family

The complete final nine-catalogue inventory contains **6,471 typed entries**, including every plural and array item: en-001, en-AU, en-CA, en-GB, en-IN, en-SG, en-US, en-XV and qaa-Zsye-XV. Full source comparison and classification accounts for 5,566 exact-source entries, 103 regional spelling variants, 52 independently assessed semantic regional variants, 713 decorated-English entries, 18 additions and 19 repairs. Nothing remains unclassified. Before the additions, 6,453 existing entries were covered by this comparison. These repeated English inventories are not 6,471 unique translations or independent revalidation of every default-English product claim.

All substantive regional differences and decorated-English exceptions were read, not inferred acceptable merely from an English-language tag. Regional synonyms such as Pipette, Spray can, Resume drawing and Per cent were retained where they match the actual operation. The independent pass caught manual references incorrectly changed to Colour/Swap colours while the actual en-CA/en-GB controls say Color/Swap colors, and en-AU references that needed Resume drawing and Per cent. Those were corrected before acceptance. All 37 proposed changes passed the independent content and structure review.

The nine long manuals were stale retained resources: **no direct production consumer was found**. Current Help uses `ui_help23`, so this is source-text maintenance, not a claim that live Help displayed the obsolete manual. Actual gallery labels now distinguish copying attribution from importing/using an image. The quality control explicitly shows its percent unit. The two qaa-Zsye-XV Commons notices retain their existing decoration and add the complete failure reason instead of relying on icons alone. Decorative English is not a claim to encode arbitrary prose solely in emoji.

### Zhuang

All **72 additions** were checked against English, actual consumers and source-backed lexical senses, with a separate integration review. **70 related existing entries** were rechecked with individual dispositions. This overlaps prior review cohorts and is not added to earlier counts as 70 unique new reviews. It does not claim that every existing Zhuang entry has completed the release review.

The wording uses authored functional descriptions where a conventional technical term could not be established. Identifiers such as pixel, font, metadata, colour profile, watercolor, vector, dithering and control characters are explicit borrowed identifiers accompanying the descriptions, not purported standardized Zhuang terms. Overall semantic confidence is bounded/moderate; source-backed components do not certify native idiom or software usability. Whole-sentence attestation and native-speaker approval were not imposed as artificial gates.

Material checks and corrections:

- **Dragging versus rolling:** four new crop/navigation strings use dictionary-supported `rag` for drag/pull, replacing a proposal based on `ringx` (roll). Existing list-scroll descriptions using ringx were retained under their separate plausible scroll sense.
- **Geometry and tools:** rectangle describes four right angles; polygon describes joined straight lines without incorrectly requiring a closed path. Solid fill names foreground interior fill without an outline, not a guarantee of opacity. The eraser names a wiping/rubbing tool. Watercolor describes the blurred-edge brush plus its technical identifier rather than saying merely colour of water.
- **Clipboard, dimensions and text:** Copy explicitly identifies making an identical copy for subsequent Paste, distinct from Save or tracing. Percent is a per-hundred ratio; pixels are image-colour points, not physical screen size. Font family is distinct from bold/italic; line spacing describes line-to-line distance, including vertical-column use. RGB inversion states the 255-minus-original channel operation and does not invert alpha or swap foreground/background.
- **Recovery and memory:** active recovery messages retain possible damage/unreadability, both ZIP readability and readable-canvas conditions, unverified ancillary data, retention even when unopenable, and unchanged raw export. They do not turn a successful byte copy into validated archive contents. Memory warnings keep all dimensions/budgets and reserve distinctions; sampled decode preserves the original file while warning of detail loss in the editable copy.
- **Formats and filenames:** PDF remains 144 dpi with vector content becoming pixels. GIF remains a still, maximum-256-colour result; the authored visual-smoothness wording describes gradual colour regions and dithering, not blur. Animated GIF import remains first-frame-only. The filename warning rejects slash, backslash and the complete non-display control-character class, not merely newline/tab or all non-Latin characters. Two raw Android backslashes yield one displayed backslash.
- **Existing text terminology:** `Vwnz` and `Gya vwnz` remain unchanged with explicit lexical uncertainty in this cohort; they are not declared wrong solely because related strings use `cih`.

Long composed labels, technical identifiers and warnings need installed small-screen/large-font and accessibility checks. No native-speaker certification, glyph/layout or TalkBack acceptance is claimed.
