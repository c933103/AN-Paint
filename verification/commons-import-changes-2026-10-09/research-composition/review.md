## Alpha semantics

[W3C simple alpha compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing) supplies the semantic reference: source-over combines source and backdrop contributions using alpha. With opaque white backdrop, the conceptual colour result is `alpha × source + (1 − alpha) × white` and output alpha is opaque. This is a semantic reference, not an implementation or colour-space test.

Every candidate names both image and background (or their colours), has a combining/mixing predicate, and gives alpha an instrumental or basis role. None asserts channel deletion, only painting fully transparent pixels, changing opacity, or equal-weight averaging. Ordinary “mix/combine using alpha” is adequate for this short explanation: the mixing verb alone does not mandate equal proportions, and alpha supplies the controlling parameter. No extra formula, new technical jargon, or new glyphs are needed.

## Complete-message decisions

### ain-Kana / ain-Latn

Retain. The parse is `[alpha ani] [[noka kor iro] newa [oka iro]] a=ukopoye`: parameter adjunct, two colour objects, then a transitive predicate with generic/inclusive agency. The SVG phrase is the original-size label rather than a resizing instruction. The Kana and Latin messages have identical words and roles, including `a=ukopoye` / `アウコポイェ`.

Independent [museum ukopoye](https://ainugo.nam.go.jp/search_ajax/dict?word=ukopoye) lookup confirms transitive mixing and its Kana spelling. [ani](https://ainugo.nam.go.jp/search_ajax/dict?word=ani) explicitly has a by/according-to sense, so it need not suggest physically stirring with an alpha object. [Tamura's museum grammar](https://ainugo.nam.go.jp/pages/ainu_basic.html) supports pre-verbal arguments and generic `a=`. [hoski](https://ainugo.nam.go.jp/search_ajax/dict?word=hoski) supports prior/first usage, but does not establish this exact dimensions compound.

Remaining uncertainty: `hoski pororu` and `oka iro` are retained catalogue terminology; `pororu` is a documented coinage in the specialist evidence, and `oka` has ordinary behind/after senses. That does not establish community acceptance of these computing compounds. No better independently supported full replacement was established here. Do not revert to unattested `a=ukomare` merely to follow the old catalogue.

### bo

Retain. `[alpha གཞིར་བཟུང་ནས]` scopes over mixing the coordinated image/background colour objects. The final བ is an operation nominal suitable for the parenthesis, not a command to remove alpha. The SVG noun phrase refers to initial/original dimensions.

The Tibetan basis construction was questioned independently. The [published Tibetan measurement article](https://magazine.zangdiyg.cn/articles/9254) has a noun phrase immediately followed by གཞིར་བཟུང་ནས. Therefore insertion of ལ after alpha is not a demonstrated mandatory correction. The [Delhi-hosted authored textbook](https://www.du.ac.in/du/uploads/departments/BuddhistStudies/Study%20Material/21052020_BS201_a-textbook-of-tibetan-language-lama-choedak-t-yuthok_1.pdf) has indexed བསྲེ lexical evidence; that indexed table is not glyph-image verification.

Remaining uncertainty: brevity of རྒྱབ་ལྗོངས་ཁ་དོག without an explicit second genitive and the overall technical register. The object relation is recoverable, and no source established that a genitive insertion is required.

### dz

Retain. `[alpha ལུ་གཞི་བཞག་སྟེ]` gives the basis of the following action. Two colours joined with དང are the mixed inputs. The ནི form functions as an operation label. This is Dzongkha composition, not replacement by the Tibetan sentence.

The [DDC 2023 dictionary](https://www.dzongkha.gov.bt/uploads/files/publications/Eng-Dzo_Dictionary_2023_3ead53caad0798894c3908a9aedceb84.pdf), indexed printed p.1120, independently confirms the chosen mix entry. [Official LibreOffice help](https://help.libreoffice.org/latest/dz/text/scalc/01/04060106.html) independently attests the basis construction with a numeric parameter (LN section). The full DDC PDF did not load in this pass; no complete PDF/glyph review is claimed.

Remaining uncertainty: the short compound for background colour and the complete parenthetical UI register, not the role of alpha or the mixing predicate.

### hak-Hant-TW / hak-Latn-TW

Retain. `[照 alpha] [混合 [[圖像个色水] 摎 [背景色]]]` gives basis, action and its two coordinated objects. 个 makes the first possessive relation explicitly Hakka, and the complete structure keeps the two colours as inputs. The Latin pair encodes the same lexemes in the same order; it is not a Mandarin reading of the Han text.

The [MOE 照 entry](https://hakkadict.moe.edu.tw/search_result/?id=16738) independently confirms the according-to sense. The [MOE 摎 entry](https://hakkadict.moe.edu.tw/search_result/?accent=4&id=11325) and [MOE recommended Hakka characters](https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/files/site_content/M0001/language_112/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/%E7%AC%AC1%E6%89%B9%E6%8E%A8%E8%96%A6%E7%94%A8%E5%AD%97.pdf) support its conjunction function; dialect-specific romanization is not mechanically converted to PFS.

Remaining uncertainty: PFS tones and orthography across this full technical sentence, and naturalness of the long postverbal object, were not established by an independent PFS source. The Han/Latin alignment itself passes. The specialist's educational 混合 source was not independently recovered in this pass. Taiwan Southern Min dictionary hits returned by search were excluded, even where the Han characters matched.

### jje

Retain. `[alpha를 기준으로] [[이미지 색]이영 [배경색]을] 허끄기` gives a basis phrase followed by coordinated objects and an action nominal. The accusative on the final object is consistent with the coordinated reading; nothing in the note commands opacity adjustment. The short size label does not force a Korean-language sentence.

The [Jeju Studies Research Center entry](https://www.jeju.go.kr/jedu/data/card.htm?act=view&page=14&seq=2015935) independently confirms the Jeju mixing lemma 허끄다. This is stronger than simply replacing a Korean polite suffix, but it does not certify the entire new message.

Remaining uncertainty: technical loans, the nominal UI register, and the complete coordination construction remain source-assisted composition rather than native acceptance. No independent source-backed replacement was established.

### mn-Mong

Retain. `[alpha ᠶᠢ ᠠᠰᠢᠭᠯᠠᠵᠤ]` is a using-alpha clause; `[image colour ᠪᠠ background colour] ᠶᠢ` is the object of combining. Alpha is not the object being combined with the image: it is the object of the distinct using verb. The initial size phrase is a noun phrase in the parameter list.

[Mongoltoli НЭГТГЭХ](https://mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/65225) independently prints the exact Traditional Mongolian predicate and supports combining separate things. The catalogue already supplies the using construction and colour terms. No Cyrillic-to-Traditional conversion was performed.

Remaining uncertainty: final accusative scope over the coordinated colour phrase, adjective/compound register of original size, and full technical idiom. The retrieved dictionary validates the predicate, not this entire clause. Spacing/shaping is outside this linguistic check.

### mnc-Mong

Retain. `[alpha i songkoi] [[nirugan i boco] jai [amargi boco]] be suwaliyara` makes alpha the basis, both colours the coordinated object, and mixing the operation. The predicate is not an imperative to remove or alter an alpha channel. `da kemun` remains preferable to importing the catalogue's disputed adverbial `daci` as a noun modifier.

[Takekoshi's authored grammar study](https://kodaimoji.chowder.jp/pdf/pdf11/takekoshi139.pdf), PDF pp.3–4, independently supports `be`, non-perfective participles and the mixing lemma. The prior Manchu research identifies genitive + songkoi from an authored grammar; this pass treats that as prior constituent evidence, not accepted fluency. The publicly available [Möllendorff grammar transcription](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Manchu_Grammar) was also inspected, but it did not independently supply the desired songkoi entry in the retrieved text.

Remaining uncertainty: the UI use of a bare participial process label and the modern numerical application of songkoi. No evidence justified a different whole message.

### ryu

Retain. `[alphaっし] [[画像ぬ色]とぅ[背景色]] 混じゆん` is parameter/instrument, coordinated colours, then transitive mixing. This dictionary-form process description is intelligible in a compact note; it does not require adding a polite sentence ending merely to resemble other messages.

The [NINJAL OMOLO official list](https://ninda.ninjal.ac.jp/s/omolo/item-set/905511?page=284) independently identifies 混じゆん / まじゆん as transitive mixing. The verb is not Japanese intransitive 混じる.

Remaining uncertainty: zero object marking, the catalogue's modern loans and whole-message naturalness. The individual dictionary headword does not certify these features.

### vi-Hani

Retain. Read the phrases as `kích thước gốc` and `Kết hợp ảnh và nền dựa trên alpha`. The latter is ordinary Vietnamese order: combining the two named inputs on the basis of alpha. It is not Chinese syntax read with Vietnamese character names.

[Chunom 結](https://chunom.org/pages/7D50/) explicitly attests the kết hợp compound; [Chunom 𨕭](https://chunom.org/pages/2856D/) independently supplies dựa trên as a compound. The stronger contextual compound evidence avoids the unresolved 蹺 = theo mapping. No font editing is warranted by this review.

Remaining uncertainty: context-dependent Nôm readings of inherited characters and conventionality of this modern technical register. Chunom is lexical evidence, not an official modern computing terminology standard. Font coverage or an XML pass cannot settle that.
