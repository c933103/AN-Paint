# Commons import notes: 11 higher-risk locales

## Result and boundary

`proposals.json` supplies 22 complete source-assisted message candidates for all 11 requested tags. They are ready for an independent composition review, not accepted translations. No production file, prior evidence, or font was edited. The repository's withdrawn-status notice is not superseded by this research.

The SVG note is a parameter list with one translated noun phrase. Raster notes use ordinary target-language syntax to say that the image/background colours are combined using or based on **alpha**, retaining alpha as the technical coefficient label. This avoids pretending that an unattested specialized noun is established terminology. Retaining this international technical label does not leave the English phrase “alpha compositing” untranslated.

The semantic reference is [W3C Compositing and Blending, §5.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing): source and backdrop colours contribute according to their alpha values. In this app the backdrop is fixed opaque white. The proposals do not claim equal blending, editing opacity, deleting alpha, or preserving a transparent result. They do not need to reproduce the formula to describe this operation.

## Deliberate exclusions

- Unverified Ainu ukomare was replaced with attested ukopoye; no claim is made that the former is impossible or invalid everywhere.
- Tibetan and Dzongkha are not interchangeable, despite sharing script.
- Jeju is not replaced by the Korean-language parenthetical 알파 합성.
- Manchu and Mongolian use independently identified syntax and script forms.
- Nôm 蹺 as theo was not confirmed by the inspected Chunom entry (which lists nghều). The supported dựa trên compound avoids that unresolved mapping. Failed Nom Foundation lookups add no lexical evidence. Existing transparency characters were inspected but are unnecessary in this candidate.
- No codepoint was added to a font. No native, visual or Android rendering approval is inferred from string/token/font tests.
