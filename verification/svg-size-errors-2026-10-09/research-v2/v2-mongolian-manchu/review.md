# Traditional Mongolian and Manchu SVG-size messages: source review

## Result

No complete new translation is responsibly supported by the inspected evidence. Both resource values remain null for both locales. No resource file or original proposals.json was edited, no mechanical Cyrillic conversion was used, and no native review is claimed.

The useful advance is independent evidence for specific words and grammatical structures, particularly Manchu completed-action negation. The JSON evidence map separates these from unresolved full clauses.

## Traditional Mongolian (mn-Mong)

The Academy dictionary independently supplies traditional-script lemmas for specifying/defining, replacing and changing. [ТОДОРХОЙЛОХ](https://mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/86596), [ОРЛУУЛАХ](https://mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/68953), [ӨӨРЧЛӨХ](https://www.mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/72615).

Its size entry supports length/width/height semantics, but the traditional spelling was not exposed by the successful indexed retrieval. The existing script spelling is catalogue evidence, not newly verified orthography. [ХЭМЖЭЭ](https://mongoltoli.mn/dictionary/detail/120047).

The prior no-resize fragment remains catalogue-derived. A nominal no-change clause is not, by itself, evidence that the completed operation did not resize. Do not claim that removing the automatic modifier establishes finite past-event semantics. The general change verb is independently supported; the correct full traditional-script sentence is not.

Unresolved: the usable original dimension declaration; size derived from the canvas and substituted in its place; the Android bitmap dimensional bound; a natural completed negative resize clause. General dictionary senses do not prove the assembled sentences.

## Manchu (mnc-Mong)

The retained fragment “amba ajige be halahakū” now has independent support for its object marker/order, general change verb and completed negative morphology. That makes it better grounded than a deletion-only catalogue argument. The specialist teaching source differentiates completed negative forms from prohibitions and not-yet constructions. [Porter, Lesson 11](https://www.manchustudiesgroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Lesson-11-Negation.pdf), [Elliott, Verbs](https://www.manchustudiesgroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Handout-3-Verbs.pdf).

The exact size collocation remains catalogue-derived: the search found a matching gloss in an unattributed mirror, which was not accepted as authoritative validation. The accessible historical grammar glosses kemun as measure and analyzes daci as an adverb. Accordingly, the catalogue phrase daci amba ajige deserves constructional review; the evidence does not justify declaring it wrong or silently replacing it. [Möllendorff, A Manchu Grammar](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Manchu_Grammar).

An aligned Manchu text supplies an ordinary replacement usage, orolome buki, in a passage about obtaining replacement thread. It does not establish the application to a canvas-derived dimensional fallback. [Porter and Fitzgerald, The Injustice of the Silk Thread](https://www.manchustudiesgroup.org/2015/09/17/the-injustice-of-the-silk-thread/).

Unresolved: a declaration predicate appropriate for an SVG document; usable-original-dimension syntax; drawing-area-derived replacement semantics; bitmap terminology and the Android dimension-limit construction. No Mongolian text was substituted for Manchu.

## Source access and limitations

The Tohoku University documentation identifies several especially useful dictionaries, including Lessing, the Inner Mongolia University Mongolian–Chinese dictionary, An Shuangcheng’s Manchu–Chinese dictionary, and Qing dictionaries. The linked search application timed out, so this pass has no results from those dictionaries. [System documentation](https://www.cneas.tohoku.ac.jp/unit/kurib25/).

Buleku returned a JavaScript shell. Manc.hu exposed its public landing page but no lemma/corpus results; the documented lexical API needs a token. No login or credential access was attempted. [Buleku](https://buleku.org/), [Manc.hu](https://manc.hu/en), [API documentation](https://docs.buleku.org/fetch-lemma.html).

The government-announced Mongolian dictionary service failed to load through both HTTP and HTTPS. Some Mongoltoli direct pages timed out despite readable indexed excerpts. These are retrieval failures, not evidence that the requested words do not exist. [Service announcement](https://www.neac.gov.cn/seac/xwzx/201909/1137258.shtml).

The comparative negation article endpoint returned an anti-bot page. Poppe’s grammar was identified through its publisher and a restricted repository record, but its grammatical text was not inspected. No restricted access was bypassed.
