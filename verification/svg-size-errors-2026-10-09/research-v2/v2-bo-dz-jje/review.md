# v2 Tibetan, Dzongkha and Jeju review-only candidates

Two complete compositional pairs (bo/dz) and one Jeju size-limit message are proposed for review. None is accepted or production-ready. The original 48-pair snapshot and resource files are unchanged.

The absence of a dictionary’s complete UI sentence is not itself a blocker: the candidates compose supported ordinary language. The remaining gates below concern exact meaning, idiom and grammatical scope. No neighboring-language substitution or independent native review is claimed.
