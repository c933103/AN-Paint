# Ainu and Okinawan SVG-size messages: second research pass

No complete new translation is approved. All six full-message fields remain null. Existing resources and the original proposals file were not edited. No native review took place.

This is a bounded evidence result, not a claim that these languages cannot express the messages. Compositional translation does not require an exact UI sentence in a corpus. The remaining questions concern the actual terms, argument structures and negative scope.

## Useful new evidence

### Ainu, Latin and Kana

- The [National Ainu Museum grammar](https://ainugo.nam.go.jp/pages/ainu_basic.html), sections 2–4, confirms preposed negative somo, the easkay/eaykap ability pair, SOV ordering, poro “be/become big” and the absence of inflectional past tense. A past event need not be forced into a fabricated past-tense form.
- The [NINJAL corpus grammar](https://ainu.ninjal.ac.jp/folklore/) identifies a= as a fourth-person transitive subject marker with several functions. It must not be treated as a universally interchangeable passive marker.
- The [independent Modern Ainu Dictionary](https://ss1.xrea.com/toracatman.s324.xrea.com/ainu/dictionary.html) directly documents pororu “size”, with poro + ru and a coinage label. This is a documented existing neologism, not a word invented by this research pass. Coinage alone does not disqualify it. Community acceptance and its technical dimensional sense remain unverified.
- A [Hokkaido Ainu Culture Research Center article](https://ainu-center.hm.pref.hokkaido.lg.jp/kankou/kiyou/pdf/kiyou15-02.pdf), PDF page 12 / printed page 49, distinguishes itasare, tasare and utasare in trade/exchange contexts. That evidence does not yet settle the argument structure for substituting a canvas-derived size.

Retained partial terminology: Hoski pororu / ホㇱキ ポロル, from the catalogue. The size noun now has independent lexical documentation. The original/intrinsic sense of the complete phrase is still unverified.

### Okinawan

The [NINJAL Okinawan dictionary dataset](https://mmsrv.ninjal.ac.jp/okinawago/) supplied directly inspectable Shuri data. Focused entries and worksheet coordinates are in the accompanying JSON:

- kee=juN, transitive “change”, printed p.318, row6382
- mutu, “original/originally”, p.397, row8588
- ka=cuN, “write/draw”, p.300, row5826; also [individual OMOLO entry](https://ninda.ninjal.ac.jp/s/omolo/item/940437)
- magisaN, “big”, p.358, row7470, and haba, “width”, p.198, row2851
- saNtaN, “did not do”, in ordinary examples on pp.243 and584

[Miyara's practical dictionary text](https://repository.ninjal.ac.jp/records/3227), example 4898, distinguishes permitted and impermissible baggage sizes and weights. This provides an ordinary-language size-limit construction to investigate instead of importing Japanese 上限 mechanically.

Retained partial terminology: 元ぬサイズ, a defensible compositional candidate combining the catalogue loanword with documented original/originally vocabulary and Okinawan noun modification. This is not native-reviewed acceptance.

The catalogue's no-resize sentence restricts the claim with 自動っし. The new source says no resizing of any kind. Removing that modifier fixes that semantic restriction but does not by itself validate the remaining polite nominalized construction. No new full no-resize clause is approved here.

## Exact unresolved work

For both languages:

1. Express that usable original dimensions are not specified by the SVG, rather than saying only that the SVG or a number is absent.
2. Express that dimensions obtained from the canvas were not used instead, with the canvas-derived relationship and substitution arguments clear.
3. Compare original dimensions against the Android bitmap dimensional ceiling, without turning the limit into RAM, byte size or generic largeness.
4. Negate the completed size-change operation completely, without restricting the claim to automatic resizing or shrinking.

For Ainu, independently establish the canvas term and the size-change predicate used in the actual sentence. For Okinawan, establish the completed polite negative construction and the technical dimension-limit comparison. The paired Ainu scripts must represent the same validated wording.

## Retrieval limits and audit files

review.json contains each attempted source URL, access outcome, clause evidence and retained partial phrases. Some web reads returned HTTP429, HTTP502 or dynamic shells. Official NINJAL XLSX and practical-dictionary text were subsequently read successfully through their published download URLs. A generic execution cancellation was reconciled and retried; it was not attributed to the user.

The dictionary and corpus introduction pages establish source provenance and grammar, not new-translation acceptance. Literal unsuccessful searches are not evidence that a word or construction does not exist.
