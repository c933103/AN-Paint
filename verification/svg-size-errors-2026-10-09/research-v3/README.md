# Final six-pair research and source-assisted review

The authoritative published literals are [added-proposed-values.json](added-proposed-values.json).
Independent reviews are [Jeju](jeju-independent-review.json) and [the other five tags](five-locale-independent-review.json).
They are self-contained: source URLs, exact clauses, corrections, limitations and
input hashes do not require the local bulk captures to understand the result.

Earlier candidate reports are preserved as **pre-review research snapshots**:
- [Jeju](jeju-research.md), [first](jeju-corpus-attempts.json) and [second](jeju-corpus-attempts-2.json) corpus retrieval ledgers
- [Manchu](manchu-research.md)
- [Traditional Mongolian](mongolian-research.md), [source attempts](mongolian-sources-attempted.json)
- [Ainu and Okinawan](ainu-okinawan-research.md)

Their candidate wording and statements that review is pending are historical.
The independent review supplies the corrected Ainu/Okinawan values used by this
revision. Research-input filenames mentioned in prose describe temporary inputs;
they are not claims that every raw capture is distributed here. Bulk corpus HTML,
full research PDFs and complete aligned catalogue copies are intentionally omitted.

[publication-evidence-sha256.json](publication-evidence-sha256.json) distinguishes
original research-input hashes from actual published-copy hashes. Temporary
workspace prefixes were normalized for portability; original input identity is
retained separately. No native-language, complete-catalogue, Android or accessibility
acceptance is claimed by these source-assisted reports.
